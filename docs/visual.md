# Visual design

How the desk looks, and how a design change gets in: the brand, the visual system and the layout as they are, then the process for taking in a design handoff and for checking a change. What each page does and says is [ui.md](ui.md); the product principle and the rules are [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), and they win over anything here.

Most of what's below came from the inbox design kit the owner brought on 2026-11-07, which was written without looking at this repository. It was mapped onto the desk's real states and actions (the table under The kit and the desk), and the owner decided where the two disagreed:

1. **The list beside the review.** With nothing open, every list page is the full-width table, one line a row. A review or an agent's question in a wide window keeps the list it came from beside it, two lines a row, foldable to a rail; narrower, the review takes the width, with Back in its head.
2. **The kit's petrol palette is Light and Dark.** The other seven themes stay as they were.
3. **No top bar, icon A in petrol, and the name stays Review Desk** (from the kit, not contested). The owner renamed it Inbox on 2026-11-09, the kit's own word: the brand row now reads Inbox, above the Inbox page.

## The brand

The mark is the kit's icon A: a broad tray with sloped inner shoulders and a short pill floating above it, two filled paths in a 64×64 viewBox.

- One geometry: `assets/brand/mark.svg` (`currentColor`) is the master, and everything else is drawn from its two paths.
- Never redraw, stretch, stroke, shadow, gradient or badge the mark. Keep its viewBox: the silhouette spans x 8–56 and y 19.6–44.5, and the room around it is deliberate. A status dot goes on the tile's corner (the tray's alert), never on the mark.
- Petrol `#365D60` tiles with a warm-white `#F7F6F2` mark, the same in every theme. In the window the mark is `--accent-ink`: petrol in Light, a light petrol in Dark, each theme's own accent elsewhere. A coloured mark on a light surface, a light one on a dark surface, never the dark petrol on a dark panel.
- In the window it's `Mark` (`web/src/components/Mark.tsx`): inline SVG at 24px (a silhouette about 18×9px, so it holds its own beside the 15px name), `aria-hidden`, beside the word "Inbox" in the sidebar's brand row, right above the Inbox page's row. In the macOS app the traffic lights share that row and the mark stands alone (the menu bar names the app); at 641–1000px the word goes and the mark stays.

`npm run icons` (`scripts/icons.ts`, run in Electron, which rasterizes) reads the paths from the master and writes every other file:

- **Colour carries information.** Red fails, amber cautions, green is done or fine, always with its words ("CI failing", "changes requested"); the brand colour never stands in for them. Everything else is ink and pencil: what a Merging row waits on, an AI review's failure (it's about the agent, not the PR), the judge's.
- **Type.** Instrument Sans for the UI and JetBrains Mono for code, both bundled. A row's title 15px (`--title-size`), its facts 13px (`--meta-size`); the sidebar's items 13.5px; the PR, Issue or Question label 11.5px semibold; a head's name 15px semibold; a plugin part's Automations heading 15px/650, a view's name 18px/650, a label 14px/550, its help 13px, the heads in Searches and rules' rail 14px/650; a review's title 22–26px with the window. Sentence case, no all-caps, no monospace outside code. The kit's 14px titles weren't taken.
- **Selection and focus.** The table row you opened, right-clicked or Tab-focused is a quiet fill (in Light and Dark the sidebar's cool `--side-on` mixed into `--sheet`, in the other themes `--ink` mixed into it, so it follows each) with a 2px `--accent` mark at its leading edge, down all the row shows; the petrol lives in the mark and the call to action, so a tinted one still reads on it (there with an edge of `--accent`, since in some themes the fill is as dark as the wash). Keyboard focus is its own 2px `--accent-ink` ring round the row, selected or not; elsewhere `:focus-visible` is a 1.5px pencil outline. Nothing is selected when a page opens.
- **People.** A GitHub person named in a row's "by" column, a review's head, a conversation or the Reviewers part has their picture before the name: `Avatar` (`web/src/components/Avatar.tsx`), round, sized to the words (16px at a row's 13px), no ring or colour round it, inside the column so the columns still line up. None for you, a bot, a team or an agent's session name. One that doesn't load is gone, leaving no box and reserving nothing; it's `avatars.githubusercontent.com/<login>?s=40`, which the shots serve themselves (`offline()` in `scripts/shots.ts`), one login failing.
- **Space and shape.** The kit's 4px base (8, 12, 16, 24, 32) where you touch spacing; don't restyle what you don't touch. Controls take 7–9px corners; a list has none: it runs edge to edge. Motion is a spin while syncing or sending and a slow breath on loading bars, cut to nothing under reduced motion.
- **A new theme** is a row in `src/themes.ts`, a block in `styles.css` that sets every token, and its id in `index.html`'s pre-paint list. `web/test/themes.test.ts` holds the three together and every theme to contrast floors (ink 10:1 on the sheet, secondary text and labels about 4.5:1, a hairline seen but quiet): tune the colour, never the test.

- **The sidebar**: the brand row (52px, the desktop app's drag handle; 86px of left padding for the macOS traffic lights), the pages (Inbox with its count, Blocked, FYI, a hairline, Merging, the Archive), then the footer, bottom up: Settings, Ask your agent. The pill marks only where you are.
- **The page's head**: sticky, 52px, as tall as the brand row and the list's head, on `--paper` with a hairline under it, the drag handle with its controls excepted. It holds only the page's own: a list's filter (on the rows' text edge), Merging's and a Settings part's name (on the rows' text), a review's, a question's or a Settings view's Back ("‹ Inbox", "‹ Question", "‹ GitHub", "‹ Your automations"). Settings has no tabs: in Settings the sidebar lists its parts in the pages' place, the one you're on its neutral pill, under a quieter **‹ Inbox**, the plugins' under a hairline as Merging is.
- **A list page** (the Inbox, Blocked, FYI, Merging, the Archive) is one `--sheet` from the sidebar's divider to the window's edge, as the list beside a review is: no border at the table's sides and no corners, a hairline between rows and above and below the table (none above it right under the head, whose own hairline is there). The Inbox is one table with no headings; the other pages' groups (Later, Blocked, Archive…) have theirs as section headings on the rows' text, clearly further from the table above than from their own. The rows, the headings and the lines between them (Your agent, the fold, "n more reach you…") start on the page head's padding, 24px (12px at 640px and below; 16px beside a review), so the filter's edge, Merging's name and the rows' text make one line.
- **The list beside a review** is the page it was opened from (a question's is the Inbox), from the same data: its order, its groups' headings where it has them, and its filter. Two lines a row: the title, cut at two, then one pencil line (a long repo gives way first, then a sorted-away row's why, then the last fact, each to a few letters, never to a bare ellipsis), with at most one indicator, the call to action before the age so the ages end in one column. Its chevron sits on its right divider just under the heads (on their hairline where the list starts with a row, not a heading: the Inbox's, Merging's), always there, a 28px target across the divider, clear of both heads (the drag handles) and of Back ("Hide the list", "Show the list", `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`); folded is kept per device (`desk.list`). With the chat open in a window under 1600px it folds to its rail without changing that choice. It's a sibling of the page's column in `App.tsx`, never wrapped round the review: so folding, a resize or the list coming and going never draws the review again or fetches it. Keep it that way.
- **Widths at a glance**: at 640px and below the sidebar is a row and the page's padding is 12px; at 641–1000px the sidebar compacts and Ask your agent reads "Ask"; at 1100px and below the chat lies over the page; at 1160px and up a review keeps its list; under 1600px the chat folds that list. Inside a table, below 760px the stage column goes (on your own row the stage takes the place of "by you"), below 620px who it's from (your own row's changes requested and handed back stay); a repo keeps 6 letters' room and a why 10. A toast centres on the page's column, clear of the sidebar, the list and the chat, and above the bubbles when there are any.
- **Bubbles** (`Bubbles.tsx`): what's wrong with the system, a stack at the window's bottom right, 16px in, 340px wide or what the page's column leaves, left of the chat when it's open; the page's bottom padding grows by the stack's height (`--bubbles-h`), so its last rows scroll clear of them. Each is a `--sheet` panel with a hairline and `--shadow` (it lies over the page), its title in ink 13px/600, its words in pencil, a 7px dot before the title the only colour (`--red` failing, `--amber` a caution, none for news), its one fix the tinted call to action, and a pencil × to dismiss. A failed action's title is its words at 500, fading over its last second.
- **Scrolling**: the window scrolls the page, so the heads stick, `scrollIntoView` works and the shots' scroll checks hold; the sidebar, the list, the chat, a review's sidebar and the rules editor scroll inside themselves (the editor's foot, Save and the rest, sticks to the window's bottom while the editor runs past it). Code wraps. Nothing scrolls the page sideways (the shots fail on it).
- **The desktop app**: at least 760×500 (`electron/main.ts`); on macOS the traffic lights sit at 18,19 in the sidebar's brand row, and no control may sit under them at any width (the mac shots check).

## Taking in a design handoff

A handoff (a kit, a mock-up, a review of the window) is input, not law: it was likely made without this repository.

The `design-handoff` workflow (`.claude/workflows/design-handoff.js`, args `{ kit, notes? }`) does steps 1 to 4: it reads the handoff as untrusted data, maps each recommendation by area and returns the brief and the questions for the owner.

1. Read all of it, then AGENTS.md's product principle and Rules, ui.md and this file.
2. Map every recommendation onto what the desk really has: its pages, buckets and parts, a card's fields, its actions and their words. Write the mapping down, as the table above. Never invent a state, an owner, a progress or an action: where the handoff assumes one, map it honestly to what exists, or leave it out.
3. List the conflicts, with the product principle (one line a row, no chips, every action a click named in words, no key named in the window), with the Rules, and with what already works. Put each to the owner as a decision, its options and what each costs; never settle one yourself. Write the answers into the brief.
4. Write the brief: the owner's decisions, what changes and what stays in each area, starting values, and how it'll be checked. Where the brief and the handoff disagree, the brief wins.
5. Build it in steps, each committed with its tests, shots and docs: the tokens (`styles.css`, `src/themes.ts`, `index.html`'s pre-paint, the desktop window's colours, with `themes.test.ts`'s floors held); then the shell and the content (the sidebar, the heads, the columns, the rows); then the assets (the master, `npm run icons`, `Mark.tsx`); then the verification below. Keep every existing word unless the change is about words, and move a shot's assertion with the control it checks rather than deleting it.
6. Bring this file, ui.md, README and AGENTS.md up to date as each step lands.

## Checking a design change

A design change is never done from code alone: look at the shots yourself, light and dark, and say what you didn't verify.

The `design-check` workflow (`.claude/workflows/design-check.js`, args `{ base?, only?, dir? }`, `dir` for another checkout) runs the list below: the shots, a critic per group of states, a script for what the shots don't check, the diff against the design rules, and a skeptic per finding. Look at the shots yourself as well.

- **The shots**: `npm run shots` drives Chrome against the mock, so the data is the same on every run (it syncs only when a shot asks, never on a timer, so a slow run shows what a quick one does), and writes `.shots/` (`index.html` shows them all). Each shot runs in light and dark at wide 1280×820, mid 900×700, min 780×600 and mac 1280×820 (the macOS app's shell, traffic lights and all, with a stand-in for its bridge whose `status()` alone is faked, so Settings → This device has the app's rows); xl 1440×900 only for the shots that ask for it (`queue`, `settings-github`, the `beside-*` ones); and the `zoom-*` ones (`zoom-queue`, `zoom-review`, `zoom-inquiry`, `zoom-settings`) are a 1280×820 window at 200 %, 640×410 at 2x, and `zoom-512` the Inbox at 250 % (512×328), its sidebar's row scrolling to Settings. Each theme has `theme-<id>`, `-review`, `-ask` and `-inflight` at the four main sizes and `-settings` at wide; Omarchy has `theme-omarchy` and `-review` (Hackerman, a theme the desk makes from its colours), `-light` (a live switch to Nord, then to Lupine) and `-settings`. While iterating, `npm run shots -- --only queue,beside` runs the shots whose names contain one of those words; `SHOTS_MOCK_PORT` and `SHOTS_VITE_PORT` keep two checkouts' runs apart.
- **What to look at**: for a change to the shell, the rows or the palette, run them all, then look at `queue`, `review`, `beside-review`, `beside-folded`, `inquiry` and `settings-github` at each size and the `zoom-*` ones, in light and dark, and `theme-<id>` and `theme-<id>-review` for at least three other themes (a light one, a dark one and one with its own diff colours: Sand, Slate and Gruvbox, say).
- **The icons**: `npm run shots -- --only icons` writes `icons-1x.png` and `icons-2x.png`: the Mark, the app icon, the favicon, the menu bar's template (with a count) and the trays, at 16, 20, 24, 32 and 64px on the light and the dark sidebar. Judge the 1x sheet at 100 %, the 16px ones above all.
- **What the shots fail on by themselves**: the page scrolling sideways, a key chip on screen, a console error, the sidebar's or a head's words running past their edge, "Inbox" wrapping, a control under the traffic lights; the list beside a review's width, its fold button's label, state and target, and the row it marks (`beside-review`, `beside-folded`, `beside-inquiry`); a long title on one line in a table and two at most beside a review (`beside-long`); the review, its typed comment, the file being read and no new fetch surviving a fold, an unfold and a resize (`beside-keeps`); the chat folding the list without touching the saved choice (`beside-chat`); a PR's checks in its review's sidebar: closed to one line, its words and red, each check in words with a link, what's wrong first, and the facts' CI opening them (`review-checks`); that sidebar's parts in order, a line each (the stack, the checks, the reviewers, the merge, the files) with no activity, and its one icon toggle, on screen and working at the bottom of a long review, over the diff and beside it (`review-panel`), and drawn while the review loads (`review-loading`); the top of a review staying short, its empty comment box one line beside the buttons and its first file within 386px of the window's top (`review-top`); that box growing with its words while the buttons stay put, Esc leaving it, its words kept across the next item and back, and its send key (`review-comment`); the sidebar as one row at 640px (`zoom-*`); the bubbles, none while all's fine (`queue`), each one's words, colour, fix and place on the page's column (`sync-limited`, nothing to press; `sync-failed`, its Dismiss lasting a reload while the sync still fails and coming back once it recurs; `sync-offline`; `settings-github-error`; `queue-ai-paused`, its Open Settings → Agent and none on that page; `bubbles-stack`, three in order, a failed action's fading on its own and the list's last row clear of them; `bubbles-chat`, beside the chat); empty pages (`queue-empty`, `later-empty`, `inflight-empty`), loading (`queue-loading`, `review-loading`), every page ending near the window's bottom (`*-bottom`); and Settings' sidebar: where each part links, the head naming it, a draft kept across parts, Esc, the way back and the footer's Settings (`settings-nav`); GitHub's automations, their switches and a built-in's view and copy (`settings-github-automations`).
- **What they don't**, to try by hand against the mock (`MOCK_PORT=47824 node web/mock/server.ts`, then `DESK_API=http://127.0.0.1:47824 npx vite --config web/vite.config.ts`), or to script with `page.route` as the shots fake a slow desk: the desk coming back (stop the mock: the list stays, and a bubble says the desk isn't running, with Retry, as `sync-offline` shows; it must go once it's back), a failing sync (`MOCK_SYNC_ERROR=1`: the "Sync failed" bubble with the error, the list kept, never an empty Inbox; Try again reads "Syncing…" until the sync fails again), a refused write (a bubble says why and the item comes back), a refresh while you type a comment (your words stay), and the keyboard alone (Tab reaches a row and shows its ring, Enter opens it, Esc goes back).
- **Then** `npm run check`: with the lint and types it runs `web/test/themes.test.ts` (every theme's tokens and contrast) and `web/test/mark.test.ts` (one geometry).
- **Report** which PNGs you looked at, by name, and what you couldn't verify.
